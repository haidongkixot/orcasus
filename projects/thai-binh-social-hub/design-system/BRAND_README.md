Thai Binh Corporation (TBCorp) is a Vietnamese investment and trading group with 30+ years in Cuba and Latin America. This system is its corporate presentation language: confident navy and red on light, airy blue grounds, Montserrat throughout, real photography framed in circles. Slides are designed on a 1440 × 810 canvas; every size below is at that scale.

## Voice and content

- Write in English (Spanish for Cuban audiences — the anniversary mark reads "3 Décadas de Dedicación"). Speak as "we" / "our"; call the company "Thai Binh Corporation" in full on first use.
- Titles are UPPERCASE and noun-led: "THAI BINH CORPORATION / HISTORY OF FORMATION & DEVELOPMENT", "MARKET OPPORTUNITY / CUBA & BEYOND". Body copy is sentence case, warm and forward-looking: "Together, we create lasting values."
- Recurring themes: connection, dedication, intercontinental trade, well-being and prosperity. Core values: Integrity, Dedication, Creativity, Perseverance, Strength.
- Figures are concrete and sourced ("~USD 110 billion in 2024 (PPP)", then `source` style "(Cre: World bank)"). No emoji.

## Color

- Light content slides: ground `surface` (white) or `mist`; titles `heading`; subtitles `sky`; body `ink`. Panels and banner bars `ice`; ESG slide `blush`.
- Section dividers, cover-back and Thank-you use the dark theme: ground `royal`, numbers `cyan`, titles white, subtitles `sky-tint`. Solid navy panels are `navy-panel`.
- Red is the accent, never a ground for long text: `red-signal` for dots, underlines and tags; `red-headline` for red statement words; `red-block` for big blocks and the swoosh that closes sector slides; `red` belongs to the logo.
- Sector slides may borrow one sub-brand accent for their swoosh: `sector-food` (TBFood), `sector-agri` / `sector-agri-light` (TBAgri), `sector-comm` (TBComm). One accent per slide.
- `sky` (2.25:1 on white) is exact from the brand but low-contrast: keep it to 32px+ uppercase subtitles. `cyan` on `royal` is 3.3:1: display numbers only.

## Type

- Montserrat (Google Fonts; weights 400, 500, 600, 700 and italic 400) for everything; Arimo is only a fallback.
- Cover: `cover-title` / `cover-subtitle` / `cover-line` — THAI BINH / INVESTMENT TRADING / CORPORATION.
- Content slides: `slide-title` + `slide-subtitle` header, `card-title`, `year`, `lead`, `body` (15px on 18.75px), `caption` for dense timeline copy, `source` for citations.
- Dividers: `section-number` above `section-title`. Sector slides: `sector-kicker` above `sector-title`. Closing: `thank-you`.

## Layout, shape and imagery

- Titles and banner bars start at `slide-margin` (81px). The logo lockup sits top-right on every slide except full-bleed photo slides.
- The signature device is a large circle photo framed by a cyan-to-red ring that ends in the open hand of the 3 Décadas mark — on the cover, every divider and the Thank-you slide. Use the source's ring art; don't redraw it.
- Cards are white with `radius-card` corners and `shadow-card`; tags, banner bars and colour blocks are square (`radius-none`).
- Photography is real and optimistic: skyscrapers shot upward, container ships and port cranes, factories, fields, teams and Cuban students. Skies are light blue so photos blend into `mist` grounds. Sector slides end with a curved swoosh at the bottom.
- The closing community slide uses stepped squares in `navy-panel`, `navy-block` and `red-block`.

## Iconography

- The deck's icons (globe, hands, heart, factory, milestone icons) are glossy raster 3D icons in white discs, embedded in slide images; they are not included here. Supply matching PNGs; where you must substitute, use simple line icons in `navy` with `red-signal` accents and flag the substitution.
